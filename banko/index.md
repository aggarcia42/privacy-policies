# Banko privacy policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Banko is a personal finance app for Android and iOS that keeps track of your income, expenses, budgets and recurring bills.

## What Banko collects

Nothing. Banko has no user accounts, no servers and no analytics. The app never connects to the internet.

## Where your data lives

Everything you enter (transactions, categories, budgets, recurring rules and settings) is stored only in a database on your own device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

## Backups and exports

You can export a backup file (JSON) or a spreadsheet file (CSV) from the Settings screen. Those files are created on your device and handed to the app you choose in the system share sheet, for example your email or cloud storage app. What happens to a file after that is governed by the app you shared it with, not by Banko. Importing a backup reads a file you pick from your device and nothing more.

## Permissions

Banko requests no special permissions. Reading and writing backup files goes through the system file picker and share sheet, which do not give the app access to your other files.

## Children

Banko does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes its database. You can also delete individual transactions inside the app at any time.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at the same address and the date above will be updated.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at https://github.com/galirousa/banko/issues or contact the authors listed in the project README.
